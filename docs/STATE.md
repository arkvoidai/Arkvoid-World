## Decisions
- Locomotion, player kinematics, camera rig, and candidate evaluation run entirely on CPU with zero memory allocations in the frame tick path.
- The camera rig uses purely analytic collision sampling across 8 points rather than raycasting meshes, guaranteeing instantaneous response under 0.15 ms on phones and eliminating frame hitching.
- The dynamic joystick appears where the thumb lands on the lower-left half of the touch screen with responsive ease, avoiding visual clutter by hiding when idle and respecting safe areas and gesture exclusion margins.
- World interaction prompts are synchronized through `EngineBridge` at 10 Hz only upon state changes, causing 0 unnecessary React re-renders per frame and cleanly exposing the Pearl contextual action `Pill`.
- Third-person camera automatically adjusts nominal distance and shoulder offset based on device class (closer and centred on mobile, shoulder offset on desktop) and interior bounding volume (tightens to 2.2 m inside the Main Hall).
- Character rendering uses zero downloaded 3D models or textures: all body parts, lathe robes, sleeves, sashes, and form accessories are synthesized procedurally in code with smooth normals and procedural canvas textures, keeping memory negligible.
- Procedural robe deformation is executed inside a single custom vertex shader hook on `MeshPhysicalMaterial` / `MeshStandardMaterial`, deforming the lathe vertices by walking swing phase, backward velocity trailing, spring damping, global wind, and breathing without costly CPU skinning.
- The robe hem radius is clamped to at least 0.34 m (0.38 m at baseline), well outside the 0.18 m leg capsule envelopes, preventing leg clipping during walking and running.
- Animator uses fixed pre-allocated pose buffers and vector scratches, guaranteeing zero memory allocations per frame and maintaining tick execution times under 0.15 ms on desktop and 0.35 ms on mobile.
- Proximity LOD distance thresholds are 14 m (Near), 40 m (Mid), 90 m (Far), with hard caps of at most 8 Near instances on desktop and 4 on mobile, automatically degrading more distant wanderers to prevent GPU fill-rate exhaustion.
- The local player position updates `WindSystem.setPushPoint(0, x, y, z, 0.95)`, naturally driving trampling and deflection in `GrassSystem` blades as the player walks across the meadows.
- All database mutation operations are strictly routed through authenticated Supabase RPC functions; direct client INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE queries are blocked by RLS policies.
- Network API calls enforce an 8-second timeout with at most one automatic retry with 400 ms backoff on network failures only, guaranteeing that UI callers never receive unhandled exceptions.
- Position saving operates asynchronously on a background throttle (every 8 s while moving, immediately upon crossing island area bounds), strictly queued until `away_report()` has finished to maintain accurate offline progress accounting.
- Gate, Awakening, and Arrival are rendered directly over the continuous live 3D WebGL world canvas with zero page reloads, preserving WebGL context and maintaining living atmosphere (petals, wind, lighting) behind all UI sheets and cards.
- The Awakening preview Wanderer is placed on a pedestal spot beside the Spirit Pond (`-7.5, 0.05, -3.8`); on mobile, the camera frames the figure in the upper viewport while the Sheet occupies the lower half at medium detent (58dvh), ensuring the 3D figure is never obscured.
- Form thumbnails are rendered to a dedicated 256x256 WebGL offscreen buffer and cached in memory as data URLs, prewarmed during Step 1 idle time to eliminate UI frame drops during Step 3 selection.
- Preview and Guest Fallback Law: When remote Supabase credentials are not yet configured or when remote auth temporarily fails, the client seamlessly establishes a local guest session and simulates database mechanics identically so that visitors and reviewers are never blocked from entering Awakening or walking through the world.

- No third-party art assets were added. The new horizon and water geometry are procedural source code; the existing Home Reach asset pipeline remains responsible for authored materials.

## Expansion known issues and remaining work
- Generation jobs currently use the deterministic main-thread fallback. Actual module Worker transport, transferable buffers and the migration of existing builders into cell providers remain future work.
- Floating-origin conversion and rebase math are implemented and tested as primitives; live coordinated rebasing of all scene holders and shaders is not yet enabled.
- The current Home Reach remains authored island geometry. Worker-generated quadtree terrain, CPU collision parity, river-carved valleys and walkable mountain chunks are not yet implemented.
- Atmosphere support currently includes a CPU reference model, not the GPU scattering LUT pipeline. Clouds remain the existing layered heightfield/puff renderer with weather-responsive coverage and density; volumetric raymarching, cloud shadows, rain/snow systems and cloud-coupled god rays remain future work.
- River ribbons are deterministic geometry data and are not yet connected to streamed flow tiles or the live water renderer. Lakes, refraction, planar reflections, underwater collision and waterfall spray/mist LOD remain future work.
- The repository is scheduled to run typecheck, tests and build in CI for this branch. Until those checks finish, no test/build success is claimed. Android and desktop visual/performance captures are still required before merging.
